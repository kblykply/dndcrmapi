LA JOYA PERLA – Key Handover Timeline Update

We would like to share an important update regarding the key handover schedule for LA JOYA PERLA.

As outlined in Part 8 of your purchase agreement, the contractual handover date is November 2026, with an additional six-month grace period provided under the agreement.

Due to recent regional developments and their impact on the construction and development process, we will be utilizing this contractual grace period. Accordingly, we now anticipate completing the key handover process by May 2027, within the timeframe established in your purchase agreement.
